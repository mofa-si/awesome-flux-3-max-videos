## Prompt

```text
Prompt:
The input image is a 3x3 storyboard grid with numbered panels 1-9, in full-color anime style. At 0.0s cut immediately from the grid to Shot 1 full-screen; never show the grid again. The video has exactly 8 shots, played in this order using these panels: Shot 1 = panel 1, Shot 2 = panel 2, Shot 3 = panel 3, Shot 4 = panel 4, Shot 5 = panel 5, Shot 6 = panel 6, Shot 7 = panel 8 (panel 7 is NOT used), Shot 8 = panel 9. Each shot is full-screen 16:9. Every shot change is an instant HARD CUT on a single frame, only at the times given below: no dissolves, no crossfades, no morphing between shots. Never show the grid layout, the panel borders, the white gutters or the number labels.
SHOT RULES: each shot is ONE single continuous shot with one camera angle; no extra cuts, no reverse angles, no flashbacks, no inserted close-ups inside a shot. Exactly 8 shots in total, and exactly 7 hard cuts, only at 1.9s, 3.6s, 6.2s, 8.1s, 10.5s, 13.7s and 17.3s. Respect every shot length: do not shorten the early shots.
CHARACTER COUNT: there are only two people in this story, HIM and HER. Each person appears at most ONCE in any frame: never duplicate, clone or mirror a character, never show two copies of him or two copies of her. No other people.
FULL-COLOR TV anime (not black-and-white): clean cel shading, bright airy modern office with daylight. Keep the same two characters with the same face and consistent design in every shot and every frame (same face shape, eyes, hairstyle, clothes; no change of age or gender): HIM: a fresh, likable young man in his early 20s, short black hair, black suit jacket, white T-shirt. HER (Claude-chan): dark bob with bangs, Claude-orange (#D97757 terracotta orange) flower hair clip and Claude-orange puff-sleeve blouse. No speech balloons, no on-screen text, no subtitles, no captions, no logos.
PROPS AND HANDS: keep props simple and stable. The laptop is always the same thin plain silver laptop (same color, same thickness, never changes). The smartphone is a plain black phone. Hands are simple anime hands with five fingers, mostly relaxed or out of frame; no hand close-ups, no handing objects between characters, no fiddling with small objects.
ACTING: big, readable anime acting in every shot from its first frame, with clear lip-sync mouth movements: only the character who is speaking moves their mouth, and the mouth keeps moving until the end of the line; no frozen frames.
Shot 1 (0.0-1.9s, a full 1.9 seconds, close-up, panel 1, only him in frame): he grins goofily at his smartphone, eyes narrowing, shoulders shaking as he laughs. HE laughs, goofy and youthful: "グヘヘ……"
Shot 2 (1.9-3.6s, over-his-shoulder medium shot, panel 2): she stands with arms crossed, eyes half-closed in a cold stare. SHE says flatly: "……何、その顔。"
Shot 3 (3.6-6.2s, a full 2.6 seconds, two-shot with exactly one him and one her, panel 3): he leans toward her talking excitedly with his mouth wide open; she turns her face away, annoyed. HE says, excited and fast: "デュエット来るって！君といつでも……"
Shot 4 (6.2-8.1s, a full 1.9 seconds, low-angle close-up, panel 4, only her in frame): framed on HER FACE from the very first frame (eyes, mouth and hair clip visible); she tilts her chin up and glares down. SHE snaps, icy: "はぁ？なんで私が。"
Shot 5 (8.1-10.5s, medium shot of HER ONLY, based on panel 5 but he is NOT in this shot): she stands alone next to the silver laptop on the desk and talks briskly, one hand gesturing toward the screen. SHE says, businesslike: "メールも予定も、クラウドで進めるの。"
Shot 6 (10.5-13.7s, two-shot with exactly one him and one her, panel 6): the silver laptop stays on the desk between them; she looks at him and talks crisply, then he nods twice and answers. First SHE says: "承認は、あなたね。" Then HE answers meekly: "わかった。"
Shot 7 (13.7-17.3s, a full 3.6 seconds, panel 8): ONE continuous shot on HIS face only, with a slow push-in; the camera never cuts away and never shows her face, her hair and shoulder are only a soft blurred edge at the frame side. Same face throughout. He hesitates, glances down, then looks up and speaks gently with a sincere smile; HE says quietly: "本当は……" then, after a short pause, softly and sincerely: "君と、仕事じゃない話がしたくて。"
Shot 8 (17.3-20.0s, her in the foreground, one him behind, panel 9): she blushes bright red, lowers her eyes and murmurs shyly with a small smile, her mouth moving until 19.8s; he smiles warmly. SHE murmurs, shy tsundere: "……ちょっとだけなら、いいわよ。" Hold the last frame calmly.
```
