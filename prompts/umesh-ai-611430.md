## Prompt

```text
Prompt (I2V) : Create a dark, photorealistic cinematic horror sequence using the reference image as the opening shot. The mood should be beautiful, eerie, mysterious, and quietly disturbing — grounded, naturalistic horror rather than gothic or fantasy. The scene should feel like something impossible is happening in a real forest at night.

OPENING IMAGE

Begin with two glossy yellow rain boots standing alone on a muddy, puddled forest path at night. The path is framed by wet grass and swallowed by darkness in the distance. The lighting is low, cold, and moody, with deep blue-black tones, faint reflections in the puddles, and just enough light to catch the shine of the boots and the wet ground.

The camera starts low and close to the ground, positioned behind the boots.

Everything is still.

Rain falls softly. Tiny ripples move across the puddles. The forest is silent.

After a brief pause, one boot slowly lifts on its own.

Then the other follows.

The boots begin walking away from the camera, moving forward down the path with their fronts facing into the darkness ahead, as if an invisible woman is already wearing them. The camera watches from behind as they take slow, deliberate steps farther away. Each footstep lands with natural weight, softly splashing through shallow water and pressing into the mud. The movement should feel graceful, realistic, and unsettling.

THE FORMING FIGURE

As the boots continue walking away from the camera, a female figure begins to form above them. She must already be clothed from the start of the transformation.

A dark dress begins appearing immediately above the boots as a vague moving silhouette, already covering the form as it develops. The clothing becomes gradually more solid and detailed while she walks: wet fabric, soft folds, long sleeves, a simple natural drape. It should feel understated, realistic, and elegant — not gothic, ornate, or theatrical.

As the camera slowly rises, more of the woman becomes visible within the already-formed clothing: her body, shoulders, neck, hair, and finally her face. The transformation should feel seamless and uncanny, as if she is condensing out of shadow, mist, and rain while continuing to walk. No glowing effects, no fantasy particles, no stylized magic.

CAMERA MOVEMENT

The sequence should play as one smooth, unbroken cinematic shot.

At first, the camera follows from behind at a low angle, watching the boots walk away down the path. Then it slowly cranes upward, rising above and behind her while continuing to track her movement. As it ascends, it reveals more of her fully formed figure, with the yellow boots still visible beneath the hem of the dress.

By the time the camera reaches a high overhead angle, the woman is fully formed and still walking away into the darkness.

THE TURN

From above, the camera slowly pushes in.

As it gets closer, the woman seems to sense it.

She slows slightly, then turns her head first, then her shoulders, and finally enough of her body to look back up toward the camera. The turn should feel calm, graceful, and deliberate.

When she looks up, we see her face clearly.

She is naturally beautiful and fully human, with wet hair, pale skin touched by cold night light, and calm, realistic features. Nothing grotesque or monstrous. But there is something faintly unnerving in her stillness and in the way she looks directly at the camera.

As the camera continues to zoom in from above, she gives a soft, beautiful smile.

The smile should look normal and human — subtle, elegant, and controlled — but also slightly unsettling the longer we look at it. It should feel warm on the surface, yet unreadable underneath. Her eyes remain fixed on the camera, calm and knowing.

FINAL FEELING

End on the image of her looking upward, smiling softly in the middle of the wet forest path at night. The yellow boots remain visible as an important anchor to the original image. The final feeling should be mysterious, beautiful, and quietly unnerving.

SOUND AND MUSIC

Use sparse atmospheric sound: soft rain, distant wind, subtle forest ambience, and the wet sound of rubber boots stepping into mud. As the boots begin moving on their own, introduce a very low dark drone. Add faint minimal strings, distant reversed textures, and soft eerie tonal layers. Keep it elegant and restrained.

When she turns and looks up, reduce the sound slightly to create a sense of suspended stillness.

As the camera zooms in and she smiles, let a delicate but unsettling musical note emerge beneath the rain.

No dialogue. No scream. No harsh stinger. End on mystery.

EMOTIONAL ARC

stillness → curiosity → unease → fascination → quiet dread
```
